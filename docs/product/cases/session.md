### Receipt, resumption, and reversal

| Situation | Expected | Ref |
| --- | --- | --- |

| The receipt is truncated or noncanonical | refused, not repaired and not read as "nothing installed" | [An unreadable, noncanonical, or inconsistent receipt refuses](../invariants/distribution.md#distribution-an-unreadable-noncanonical-or-inconsistent-receipt-refuses) |

| A receipt names an operation inconsistent with its status or progress | refused without mutation | [An unreadable, noncanonical, or inconsistent receipt refuses](../invariants/distribution.md#distribution-an-unreadable-noncanonical-or-inconsistent-receipt-refuses) |
